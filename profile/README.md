## 🌟 What is Wizmade?

[**Wizmade**](https://wizmade.si) is an AI-native creative studio that unifies state-of-the-art visual generation models and precision editing workflows under one roof. 

Instead of juggling fragmented subscriptions, Discord bots, and complex node interfaces, Wizmade gives creators a streamlined, browser-based studio to go from initial concept draft to studio-grade imagery and cinematic motion in seconds.

## 🔄 How Wizmade Works (3 Simple Steps)

1. **Prepare Your Creative Brief:** Start with a text prompt or upload up to 8 reference images (`JPG`, `PNG`, `WebP`) to define your subject, style, or motion direction.
2. **Select Your AI Model & Parameters:** Choose from frontier models (like *Kling v3*, *Seedance 2.5*, or *Nano Banana Pro*) and configure aspect ratios (`1:1`, `16:9`, `9:16`, etc.), resolution, and duration in the Studio.
3. **Generate, Refine & Export:** Create your visual assets, iterate with built-in upscaling or restyling tools, and manage all your projects in a centralized Asset Library.
